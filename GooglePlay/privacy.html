Next Birthday Reminder — Android Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026
Developer: Oana Rinaldi
Android package: com.oanarinaldi.nextbirthday
Contact: oanarinaldi@gmail.com

Your personal data stays on your device

Next Birthday is a local celebration planner with no developer account, login, or server. Names, birthdays, optional photos, contact details, relationship labels, favorites, notes, gift ideas and history, celebration intentions and budgets, checklists, catch-up cadence, connection journal entries and preferences are saved in the app’s private storage. We do not collect or share this personal planning data.

Contacts and photos

Contacts access is optional and read-only. It is requested when you choose Import from Contacts. The app reads names and saved birthdays to build an import list, and reads associated phone numbers and email addresses for contact actions. You choose which people to import; nothing is saved until you tap Import. Contacts are never uploaded or modified. You can revoke access in Android’s app permission settings.

Optional profile photos use Android’s system photo picker. The app receives only the image you select, resizes it, and saves a copy locally. The app does not request access to your full photo library.

Notifications and widgets

Birthday reminders are scheduled locally by Android. We do not use a push notification server. Reminders may contain a person’s name and birthday; you can control notification permission and Lock Screen visibility in Android settings. Widgets read the app’s local database and open the corresponding person when tapped. Android may delay reminders or widget updates during battery saving. Lock Screen widgets depend on device support.

Backups, export, and deletion

Automatic Android cloud backup and device transfer of this app’s files are disabled. Settings lets you export and import a JSON backup compatible with Next Birthday on iPhone. Exporting uses Android’s share sheet and sends the file only to the destination you choose. Backup files can contain personal notes, contact information and photos; keep them somewhere you trust. We do not receive them. Deleting a person deletes their local notes, gifts, plans and journal entries. Uninstalling or clearing app data removes the local database. Export first if you want to keep a copy.

Optional Pro subscriptions

Google Play handles monthly and yearly Pro subscriptions, payment, restore and subscription management. The app uses Google Play Billing to load localized prices, process purchases, acknowledge transactions and verify current access. Google handles transaction and account information under Google’s Privacy Policy. We do not receive payment card information, and birthdays, contacts, plans and journal entries are not sent as part of billing. Purchase entitlement is kept separately from editable backups. When Pro ends, saved checklists and plans remain readable; their Pro editing features require active access.

External actions

Message, Call, Email, WhatsApp, sharing and web links hand off to another app. Next Birthday does not send messages or make calls automatically. Data you choose to share is handled under the destination app’s own terms and privacy policy.

No tracking or advertising

The app includes no advertising, analytics, developer crash-reporting or location SDKs. We do not sell personal data or track users across apps. Google Play and Android may process platform diagnostics and purchase information under their own policies. If you email us for support, we receive the information you choose to include and use it to respond.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Optional subscriptions require the applicable Google Play account permissions. Store age ratings and parental controls apply.
