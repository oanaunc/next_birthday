Make people feel remembered.

Next Birthday is a private celebration planner for the people who matter to you. Go beyond a countdown: turn what you know about someone into a thoughtful gesture, and remember the moments you share throughout the year.

YOUR TODAY
See the next occasion, your celebration intention and the people on your horizon in a calm, easy-to-read space.

THOUGHTFUL PLANS
Write a personal intention and set an optional budget. Keep gift ideas and a history of past gifts alongside each person's birthday.

MOMENTS TO KEEP
Save a dated note about time together. Keep the little details that make the next conversation personal.

THE BIRTHDAY ESSENTIALS
Unlimited people, optional Contacts import, local reminders, calendar, favorites, Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets, and JSON backup export/import. Birthday years are optional.

OPTIONAL NEXT BIRTHDAY PRO
An auto-renewing monthly or yearly subscription adds editable celebration checklists and a personal catch-up cadence. Choose a rhythm for each person and see due catch-ups on Today when you open the app. Both plans include the same features. Your saved checklists remain readable when Pro ends. Birthday reminders, intentions, budgets, gift ideas, journaling and backups remain free.

No developer account, advertising or analytics. Your personal notes and plans stay on your iPhone. Apple processes subscriptions. Prices and billing periods are shown before purchase. Payment is charged to your Apple Account; subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Manage or cancel in Apple Account settings.
