03App Privacy

The questionnaire that produces the privacy label. Your answer is the short one — but the reasoning matters if review queries it.

Privacy Policy URLready

Data collection

Do you collect data from this app?
No, we do not collect data from this app

Apple defines collection as transmitting data off the device. This app has no server and never opens a network connection, so nothing qualifies — including the Contacts data, which is read locally, copied into the app's own database only for the people you tick, and never transmitted. Reading data is not collecting it.

You will still be asked to confirm the app doesn't use data for tracking. It doesn't: no analytics SDK, no advertising identifier, no third-party frameworks at all.

Description4000 max2143 chars
Never forget a birthday again.

Next Birthday Reminder keeps every birthday, reminder, note and gift idea in one place — and keeps all of it on your iPhone. No account, no sign-up, no server, no ads.

UPCOMING AT A GLANCE
Open the app and the next birthday is right there, with a live countdown down to the second. Below it, everyone else is grouped the way you actually think: Today, Tomorrow, This Week, Next Week, then month by month.

IMPORT FROM CONTACTS
Bring in the birthdays already saved in your Contacts. You see exactly who will be imported before anything is added, duplicates are flagged, and nothing is written until you tap Import. Access is read-only — your Contacts are never changed.

REMINDERS THAT ARRIVE IN TIME
Pick as many reminders as you like: on the day, 1 day, 3 days, 1 week, 2 weeks or a month before. Set one time of day for all of them, and override it for anyone who deserves more notice. Early reminders nudge you about a gift; same-day ones nudge you to send a message.

WIDGETS
Up Next on your Home Screen in three sizes, a big Countdown widget for the next birthday, and Lock Screen widgets that keep the number of days one glance away.

GIFT IDEAS AND NOTES
Jot ideas down whenever they come up during the year. Tick one off when you buy it and it moves into that person's gift history, so next year you can see what you gave last time. Notes hold the small things — shirt size, favourite flowers, the thing they mentioned wanting.

CALENDAR AND PEOPLE
A month grid with a dot on every birthday, and a year view showing which months are busy. Search by name, note, relationship, month or zodiac sign. Filter by Family, Friends, Work or your own groups.

DON'T KNOW THE YEAR?
Leave it out. The countdown still works — the age simply isn't shown. Most people know the day and month and not much else, and the app is built for that.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Everything lives in the app's own database on your iPhone. Nothing is uploaded, there is no analytics SDK, and the app never goes online. Export your birthdays as a plain JSON file any time you want a copy.

What to shoot, in order

  1. Upcoming, with the hero card and a live countdown

    The one screen that sells the app. Seed it with five or six people first — an empty list photographs badly.

  2. Import from Contacts, mid-selection

    Your strongest differentiator against putting birthdays in Calendar.

  3. A person's detail page showing gift ideas and history

    Use real-looking ideas, not "test test".

  4. Home Screen with two or three widgets placed

    Take this from the Home Screen itself, not from inside the app.

  5. Calendar, year view

    Reads instantly at thumbnail size.

  6. Settings, or the Privacy page

    "Stays on your iPhone" is a selling point — show it.

Use plausible names and dates. Reviewers notice placeholder junk, and so do buyers.

Notes for the reviewerready
No account or sign-in is required. The app works fully offline and makes no network requests.

Contacts permission is optional. The app works entirely without it — birthdays can be added by hand. If granted, it reads only name and birthday fields to build the import list shown in Import from Contacts, and writes nothing back to Contacts. To test without granting permission, tap Add a Birthday on the first screen.

Widgets require the App Group group.com.nextbirthday.app, which is enabled on both the app and the widget extension. To see them, add "Next Birthday" from the Home Screen widget gallery after adding at least one birthday in the app.

Notifications are local only, scheduled through UNUserNotificationCenter. There is no push server.

The app contains no third-party SDKs, no analytics, no advertising and no in-app purchases.

Export compliance

Already declared in the build — ITSAppUsesNonExemptEncryption is set to false in Info.plist, so App Store Connect will not ask you about encryption at upload. Nothing to do.

07Order of work

Each step unblocks the next. Doing them out of order is what produced the upload error earlier.

  1. Upload the two HTML pages to oanarinaldi.com

    Open both URLs in a browser and confirm they load before going further.

  2. Register the widget App ID and the App Group at developer.apple.com

    com.nextbirthday.app.widget and group.com.nextbirthday.app, then enable App Groups on both App IDs and assign the group to each.

  3. In Xcode, set your team on both targets and confirm the App Group row is ticked

    Automatic signing should resolve with no red errors before you archive.

  4. Archive fresh and upload

    Discard any earlier archive — those carry the wrong bundle identifier.

  5. Take the screenshots while the build processes

    Processing takes 10 to 60 minutes; use the time.

  6. Fill in sections 01 to 06 above

    Everything marked ready is copy-paste.

  7. Complete the age-rating questionnaire

    Mandatory since September 2026. All answers are None or No.

  8. Attach the build, then Submit for Review

    The build only appears in the version page once processing finishes.
